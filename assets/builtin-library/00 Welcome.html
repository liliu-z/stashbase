A Wiki for your local files.

StashBase is a Wiki for your local files. Add a folder you already use, or create a new one. Your files stay where they are and remain the source of truth.

StashBase helps you browse those files, prepare difficult formats, find source evidence, and work with Claude Code, Codex, or another MCP client against an explicit scope.

Sources → Wiki Pages + Search → Agents.
Reviewed results remain ordinary files.

You do not need to read a manual.

The Chat that opened with StashBase can use the detailed guides in this folder. Try asking:

  • How do I use StashBase?
  • What works without Similarity Search or an account?
  • How is StashBase different from NotebookLM or Obsidian?

Or begin on your own:

  • Choose useful context. Open this folder from the Library menu, or use Add Folder… for one of your own local folders.
  • Build Wiki. In a folder-scoped blank Chat, choose Build Wiki to create linked pages under wiki/ without changing your Sources.
  • Reach a local first result. Open a source or use Exact Search. Browsing, preview, editing, and Exact Search need neither an account nor Similarity Search.
  • Choose Similarity Search. The first folder offers setup once. You may choose Not now and keep working locally, then return through Set up or Settings.
  • Keep the result. Ask an Agent to write only accepted conclusions into an ordinary file, then review and save it.

The other files in this folder give Agents detailed, current-at-installation answers about workflows, formats, privacy, limitations, troubleshooting, and product comparisons. This folder is ordinary user-owned content too: keep it, change it, remove it from the Library, or delete it from disk when you no longer need it.
